About Technex: Technex is the National Level Annual Technical Fest of SVPCET, recognized as Central India's largest Science and Technology Fest.
It brings together over three thousand plus technology enthusiasts for creativity, competition, and collaboration.

--- HISTORY OF TECHNEX ---
Organized and driven by a passionate team of students under the guidance of esteemed faculty mentors, Technex has been fostering innovation and technological excellence since its inception in 2007.

Over the years, Technex has grown by introducing cutting-edge events, workshops, and keynote sessions led by industry experts and innovators. These initiatives expose participants to emerging technologies, encourage critical thinking, and promote real-world problem solving, continuing to inspire the next generation of engineers.

--- WHAT IS St. VINCENT PALLOTTI COLLEGE ---
St. Vincent Pallotti College of Engineering and Technology was established in 2004 by the Nagpur Pallottine Society. The College is accredited by NAAC with A grade. The College is affiliated to Nagpur University approved by Director of Technical Education, Mumbai and AICTE, Government of India.

--- MISSION AND VISION ---
Vision : To develop a knowledge based society with clarity of thoughts and charity at hearts to serve humanity with integrity.

Mission : To empower youth to be technocrats of tomorrow with absolute discipline, quest for knowledge and strong ethos to uphold the spirit of professionalism.
